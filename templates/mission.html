LEVEL 1B · MISSION STATEMENT

Informed Communities, Healthier Futures

Global Immunisation Explorer turns raw immunisation and infection records into information that anyone — from a policy advisor to a curious parent — can actually use to understand how the world is progressing against preventable disease.

Built on Unbiased data
THE CHALLENGE

Why this site exists

Preventable-disease data is often scattered, technical or one-sided.

Vaccination protects individuals and, at scale, whole populations — but the public conversation around it is frequently shaped by anecdote rather than evidence. This site presents immunisation and infection data exactly as it is recorded, without editorialising, so every visitor can draw their own informed conclusions.

  • Present coverage, dose and case data in plain, respectful language.
  • Flag missing or inconsistent records instead of hiding them.
  • Let every user filter down to the country, region or economic group they care about.
HOW TO USE IT

Three ways to explore

Every page builds on the last, from a snapshot to a deep-dive.

  • Overview — a snapshot of the dataset: countries, antigens and the years covered.
  • Vaccination & Infection Explorers — filter coverage and case data by country, region, year or economic status.
  • Improvement & Above-Average views — deep-dive comparisons that surface the countries most worth watching.
